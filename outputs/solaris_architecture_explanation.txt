Solaris Agentic AI Architecture - 60 second explanation

Solaris has three main layers.

First, the customer layer includes the dashboard, floating agent chat, Mission Control, authentication, OTP access, and notification opt-ins.

Second, the Solaris backend keeps the system safe and operational. It manages customer-specific state, session isolation, explicit APIs, event logs, preferences, ticket history, and agent memory.

Third, the OpenAI reasoning layer powers the Solaris Orchestrator Agent. The Orchestrator does not simply answer questions. It receives a goal, observes solar and customer data, selects the right specialist agents, decides the safest action, asks for approval when needed, executes tools, verifies the result, and learns customer preferences.

The specialist agents are solar monitoring, appliance/NILM and smart plug tracking, bill explainer, surplus automation, cleaning ROI, underperformance tickets, peer benchmark, notification, and memory.

The adapter layer connects Solaris to real operations: Gmail, WhatsApp, calendar reminders, ticket workflows, cleaner control, and future inverter, smart meter, battery, weather, and tariff APIs.

The business value is operational transformation for solar customer operations: faster issue detection, fewer manual follow-ups, better customer decisions, improved solar self-consumption, and a repeatable workflow that can scale across many customers and sites.
